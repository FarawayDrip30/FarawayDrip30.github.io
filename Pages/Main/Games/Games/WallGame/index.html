WallGame

WallGame is a Game about Walls.

Meet the Cast!

WallGame


WallGame has unfortunately become insane from the tribulations of living on a planet where giant walls arise from the ground and slaughter millions, and has since lost all sense and runs around the planet freely and dangerously.

MouseGame

MouseGame is a prisoner of God and is puppeteered against his will by the omnipotent force, who will one day certainly stop taking pity on him and allow him to be claimed by the walls.

BounceGame

A psychic, one day BounceGame had a vision of a game of basketball, here on Earth, and was determined to dedicate his life to perfecting the sport through trial of fire through the walls, even if he were to die doing it.
