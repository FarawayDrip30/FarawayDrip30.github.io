Out of the Loop


You are in a party and it seems like everyone knows some secret you don't, you are...

You are a clown that has the power to possess other people, with the cost of having the day restart, you must find out the gossip and get in the loop.

A bouncer, a guy that oddly looks like a frog, an electrician, a cat, a lesbian, a vampire and other strange characters walk into a party. What do they know, can you find out?
