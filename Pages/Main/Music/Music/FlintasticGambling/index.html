Flintastic Experiences: Gambling Emporium OST

This is the soundtrack I made for my pal flines' game, Flintastic Experiences: Gambling Emporium.

It's a game based on his webseries called flintastic adventures, which uses the jazz fusion album by Himiko Kikuchi, Flying Beagle, as its soundtrack. My music was made for an update of the game, with the original using the same soundtrack, so I wanted to make a soundtrack that had a bit of swing to it and electronic so it had a similar vibe.
