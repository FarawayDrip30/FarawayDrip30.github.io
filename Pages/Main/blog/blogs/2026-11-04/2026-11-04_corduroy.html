Corduroy

Yesterday I was in one of the toilets in my university when a teacher-looking man came in. He looked pretty wise, white hair and beard and lanyard. Presentable trousers. Even had a brown corduroy overshirt. I think? I just looked up what corduroy looks like and it doesn't look like that. The memory of what his overshirt looked like has now faded. Either way, despite being in the toilet I felt like he wasn't going to use it, perhaps because of his formal appearance. However, he went and used one of the urinals, which actually surprised me. I think this can be applied to videogames somehow.
