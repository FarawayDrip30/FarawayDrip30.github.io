I love my dog Ozzy, AKA Oswald or Lord Oswald (somehow). He is quite magnificent.

Here is a poem I have written about him. Sing it to the tune of "Trash" by New York Dolls.


Oh Ozzy,
You are a dog,
Oh Ozzy,
You like to eat food.

Oh Ozzy,
You are our dog,
Oh Ozzy,
You like to eat our food,
and you like to eat it and then pretend you didn't
and you could scare off the whole world until the whole world realises you were more scared of the whole world,
and you are our dog
and you eat our food
and we can pet you and we try to play with you whenever you don't want to just go back inside
and that's you.
